# Customer report - round 1
VERDICT: COMPLAINTS
Job: TM-1007, a new 20A circuit and isolator for a heat pump in a 1960s Glengarry house with an old rewireable fuse board. Priced at $952.20, plus a $236.33 earth-rod variation (no main earth). Done over Tue/Wed evenings, CoC in, confirmed, 5 stars. It went well because the people answered in chat. The site itself held very little of what I needed.

## 1. The agreed visit time isn't on the job anywhere   [major]
Moment: Jack and I agreed Tue 27th ~5:15pm, then Wed 5:15pm for the earth.
Problem: That only exists in the chat. The status box still said "Your electrician is booked and will be in touch" after he had been in touch. The chat opens scrolled to the OLDEST message, so on my phone I'd have to scroll a small box to find when he's coming. That's the one thing I care about.

## 2. Office promised me Jack, the site said it was still "matching" me   [major]
Moment: Straight after paying.
Problem: The office told me Jack was coming Tuesday. Once I paid, the job said "Paid - we're matching you with a verified local electrician." So is it Jack or not? (The office says any sparky could have grabbed it first.) Being burnt by no-shows is exactly why I'm wary.

## 3. Nothing told me where my money goes until I asked   [minor]
Moment: Deciding whether to pay $952.20 upfront.
Problem: Neither the price nor the pay page says the money is held until the CoC and my OK. I had to message and wait for the office before I'd pay. Most people would just leave instead.

## 4. The extra cost was just a total, and my running total isn't shown   [major]
Moment: Approving the earth-rod variation.
Problem: The main price had a labour/materials/GST split. The variation only said "$236.33", with Jack's note saying "~$70" materials and "1.5 extra hrs". It didn't say whether the conduit hour I'd already paid for came off. The office had to work out the breakdown by hand in chat. After paying, the job still only shows $952.20 at the top. Nowhere does it say I've paid $1,188.53 all up, and "all up, no surprises" was the whole point.

## 5. No wrap-up from the sparky before I release his pay   [minor]
Moment: "Work done - please confirm."
Problem: Jack never posted a "done: here's what I did and tested" message. The CoC just appeared. Nothing asked about the heat pump not being installed yet (so I can't test the circuit) before confirming released his pay. It worked out, but I was confirming on trust.

## 6. I signed off a "CoC" that was really a photo of my fuse board   [major]
Moment: Confirming the job (found out later from the office's log).
Problem: The "View / download CoC" file was just the fuse-board photo. I didn't open it, because nothing asked me to check it before "Confirm job is complete" released the pay. Now the office says my job page has the wrong file and nobody can fix it. The real certificate is coming by email, off the record. That certificate is what my insurer would ask for.

## 7. My message looked like it never sent   [minor]
Moment: Sending my questions about the $236 (I clicked Approve while it was still sending).
Problem: The Send button stuck on "..." and my message didn't show until I reloaded the page. Not sure if clicking Approve caused it. On a phone I'd have typed it all again or given up.
